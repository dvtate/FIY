End Session

This will end your current session. Sessions on other devices and browsers will remain active.

Log Out

This will end all active sessions for your account, including on other devices and browsers.

Delete Account

Deleting your account is permanent. In addition to removing your login credentials, it will tell all installed apps/mods to delete any data that is associated with your account. For example, your contacts will be deleted. This will not delete other users' data. For example, if another user has a contact for you, it will not be deleted. Additionally, it is the apps responsibility to communicate your accounts' deletion to other instances. This could result in lingering traces of your account. With your account deleted there is also a risk of a malicious user attempting to impersonate you. If you still want to delete your account, we totally understand, and we're sorry to see you go.
